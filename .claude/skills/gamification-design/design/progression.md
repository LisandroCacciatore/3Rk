# Progression Design

Progression must reflect **increasing competence**, not merely time spent or numerical inflation.

## Progression Dimensions Matrix

| Dimension | Measure | Drive | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skill | Execution accuracy / Efficiency | CD2, CD3 | Unlocking advanced features/tools |
| Knowledge | Understanding of complex systems | CD2, CD7 | Data insights, deep analytics access |
| Autonomy | Ability to configure own workflow | CD3, CD4 | Custom macros, adaptive dashboards |
| Status | Recognition by peer group | CD5, CD4 | Elite tags, mentor status, badges |
| Territory | Expansion of domain control | CD4 | Team management, workspace creation |

---

## Anti-Grind Principles

1. **Meaningful Level Up:** Leveling up or reaching a milestone must alter the user's capabilities, not just increment a counter.

2. **Diminishing Mechanical Returns:** Never reward repetitive basic actions with linear point accumulation. Reduce rewards for repeated basic actions; increase rewards for novel/complex actions.

3. **Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment:** Match challenge level to skill level to maintain the **Flow State** (Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi).
   - High Challenge + Low Skill = Anxiety
   - Low Challenge + High Skill = Boredom
   - Matched Increase = Flow Zone
