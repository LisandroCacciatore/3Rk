# Core Drives Reference

Use this module to map specific product interactions to exact behavioral motivators.

## CD1: Epic Meaning & Calling
- **User Perception:** "I am acting for something bigger than myself."
- **Primary Triggers:** Narrative onboarding, elite selection, community mission, open-source contribution.
- **Key Mechanics:** Narrative, Beginner's Luck, Free Lunch, Humanity Hero.
- **Risks:** If disingenuous or purely marketing-driven, users feel manipulated.

## CD2: Development & Accomplishment
- **User Perception:** "I am making progress, mastering skills, and overcoming obstacles."
- **Primary Triggers:** Challenges, skill mastery, incremental goals, visible growth.
- **Key Mechanics:** Points, Badges, Leaderboards, Progress Bars, Milestone Unlocks, Boss Fights.
- **Risks:** Meaningless points without real effort create the PBL Fallacy.

## CD3: Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback
- **User Perception:** "I am expressing myself, experimenting with strategy, and seeing real-time results."
- **Primary Triggers:** Open-ended choices, strategy building, customization, immediate feedback loops.
- **Key Mechanics:** Evergreen Mechanics, Choice Perception, Strategy Customization, Real-time Loops.
- **Risks:** High cognitive load if introduced before the user understands basic mechanics.

## CD4: Ownership & Possession
- **User Perception:** "I control this, I built this, and I want to protect and improve it."
- **Primary Triggers:** Custom profiles, virtual currencies, collection sets, historical data accumulation.
- **Key Mechanics:** Collection Sets, Virtual Goods, Customization, Build from Scratch, Alfred Effect.
- **Risks:** Creates sunk-cost friction if users want to leave or change systems.

## CD5: Social Influence & Relatedness
- **User Perception:** "I am connected to others through competition, collaboration, mentorship, or shared identity."
- **Primary Triggers:** Social proof, mentorship, team goals, status comparison, nostalgia.
- **Key Mechanics:** Group Quests, Social Treasures, Mentorship Systems, Bragging, Water Cooler Dynamics.
- **Risks:** Social anxiety, unhealthy toxic competition, or unwanted social pressure.

## CD6: Scarcity & Impatience
- **User Perception:** "I want this because I cannot have it right now or it is rare."
- **Primary Triggers:** Time gates, limited availability, exclusive access, appointment dynamics.
- **Key Mechanics:** Appointment Dynamics, Torture Breaks, Countdown Timers, Exclusive Access.
- **Risks:** High annoyance factor if artificial; can drive users to abandon the platform.

## CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity
- **User Perception:** "I want to know what happens next."
- **Primary Triggers:** Mystery, unexpected feedback, dynamic rewards, exploratory mechanics.
- **Key Mechanics:** Mystery Boxes, Easter Eggs, Random Rewards, Visual Storytelling, Rolling Rewards.
- **Risks:** Can induce compulsive/addictive loops (Skinner Box) without true intrinsic value.

## CD8: Loss & Avoidance
- **User Perception:** "I must act now to prevent losing something valuable."
- **Primary Triggers:** Expiring streaks, loss of status, progress reset, missed opportunities.
- **Key Mechanics:** FOMO Mechanics, Progress Loss, Status Quo Sloth, Evanescence Opportunity.
- **Risks:** High stress, anxiety, burnout, and eventual complete platform abandonment.
