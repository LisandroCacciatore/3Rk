# Gamification Anti-Patterns & Red Flags

When auditing or reviewing proposals, explicitly flag and reject these anti-patterns.

## 1. The PBL Fallacy (Points, Badges, Leaderboards)
- **Symptom:** Adding XP and generic badges to a boring or friction-filled core task.
- **Root Cause:** Confusing tracking metrics with human motivation.
- **Remediation:** Remove PBLs. Re-evaluate Core Drive alignment. Fix the core loop first.

## 2. Overjustification Effect (Intrinsic Poisoning)
- **Symptom:** Paying or giving points/extrinsic rewards for an activity users already enjoyed intrinsically. When rewards stop, user behavior stops entirely.
- **Root Cause:** Overreliance on Left-Brain Extrinsic motivators (CD2/CD4).
- **Remediation:** Replace extrinsic rewards with feedback on competence (CD2) or tools for self-expression (CD3).

## 3. The Infinite Grind (Numerical Inflation)
- **Symptom:** Requiring 1,000 repetitive tasks to reach Level 20 without changing mechanics or choices.
- **Root Cause:** Lazy endgame design substituting content depth with time sinks.
- **Remediation:** Implement Milestone Unlocks, skill trees, and autonomy upgrades (CD3/CD4).

## 4. Black Hat Exhaustion (FOMO Burnout)
- **Symptom:** High short-term retention followed by sudden, complete user churn and negative reviews.
- **Root Cause:** Overuse of Loss Avoidance (CD8), Scarcity (CD6), or high-stress Randomness (CD7).
- **Remediation:** Rebalance system towards White Hat motivators (CD1, CD2, CD3) to make users feel empowered and in control.

## 5. Metric Gaming (Goodhart's Law)
- **Symptom:** Users optimizing for points/streak counters while deteriorating actual quality or business goals (e.g., submitting empty training logs to maintain a streak).
- **Root Cause:** Rewarding proxy metrics instead of intended valuable outcomes.
- **Remediation:** Reward quality or outcome milestones, not low-friction activity proxies.
