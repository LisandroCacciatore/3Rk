## Core Drive Opportunity Diagnostic Questions

Ask these 8 questions to find quick-win interventions:

1. **CD1 (Meaning):** Does the user feel like a hero on a mission, or just an administrator entering data?
   - *Opportunity:* Add narrative framing during Onboarding or link user actions to real-world impact.

2. **CD2 (Accomplishment):** Are Win-States clear, celebrated, and felt as genuine achievements?
   - *Opportunity:* Add progress bars, milestone unlocks, and visual feedback for completed effort.

3. **CD3 (Empowerment):** Can users solve problems using their own strategies and receive instant feedback?
   - *Opportunity:* Provide meaningful choices, strategy toggles, and evergreen sandbox mechanics.

4. **CD4 (Ownership):** Do users accumulate data, custom configurations, or virtual assets over time?
   - *Opportunity:* Implement custom dashboards, saved templates, and the Alfred Effect.

5. **CD5 (Social):** What becomes more valuable because other humans are present?
   - *Opportunity:* Introduce mentorship loops, group goals, peer recognition, or team progress views.

6. **CD6 (Scarcity):** Is everything available all at once, leading to choice paralysis?
   - *Opportunity:* Pacing options, time-gated milestones, or exclusive unlockable features.

7. **CD7 (Curiosity):** Is the user experience 100% predictable and routine?
   - *Opportunity:* Add unexpected insights, variable rewards, mystery unlocks, or easter eggs.

8. **CD8 (Loss):** Are users indifferent to dropping out for weeks at a time?
   - *Opportunity:* Add streaks, streak freeze mechanics, fading opportunities, or progress protection.
